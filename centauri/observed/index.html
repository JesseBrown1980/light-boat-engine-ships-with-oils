ASOLARIA / Raw Photo Tape

Three **real raw 2D FITS images** form the tape. Each is independently quantized into nine intensity bands. The tape is stacked by observed x, y, and frame time; as it plays, the screen reprojects those measured relationships into new 2D views. No gravitational particle integration or exhaustive point enumeration is used.

Use three observations of the same field with comparable image geometry, ordered earliest to latest. They must be from different exposures to measure motion. Files stay in your browser; each original is SHA-256 identified. If the photographs are unrelated or lack registration/calibration, the motion reconstruction is a hypothesis and is not evidence of physical depth.

**Disclosure:** This is a real computational replay of user-provided raw observations. The x-y-time volume is three-dimensional in *data coordinates* but is not automatically a 3D physical-depth reconstruction. Playback between recorded epochs is interpolation; the loop back is replay, not a physical prediction. The browser can be suspended by the OS even though there is no user-imposed stop timer.
