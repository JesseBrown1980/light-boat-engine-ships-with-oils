THE LIGHT BOAT — a translucent hull on liquid white

Twenty-seven blocks — 9 cubes, 9 pyramids, 9 spheres — floating on a white medium. Three closures run at once and all three are computed, not drawn: optical T + A + R = 1, buoyancy Σdisplaced − Σweight = 0, sonar ping → echo round-trip. A photon at a translucent face does exactly one of three things, so the hull is a trit machine made of light: transmit +1 absorb 0 reflect −1.
Jesse Daniel Brown (OP-JESSE) — the system, the laws, the architecture.
Solid volumes are exact: cube s³, pyramid s³/3, sphere πs³/6. The pyramid is exactly one third of the cube — the same third that fires the crank. White is the pole at infinity, never a value: the medium is drawn as the limit, not as a colour.
